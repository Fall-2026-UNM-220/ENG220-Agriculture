Project Data Sources


* Graph 1 – Median Household Income

Source: U.S. Census Bureau, American Community Survey (ACS).

Data: Median household income for Cuba Census County Division, New Mexico, and the United States.

[U.S. Census Bureau – Cuba CCD, Sandoval County, New Mexico](https://data.census.gov/profile/Cuba_CCD,_Sandoval_County,_New_Mexico?g=060XX00US3504390870#populations-and-people)


* Graphs 2 and 3 – Housing w/o vehicles (receiving SNAP)

Source: USDA Economic Research Service, Food Access Research Atlas.

Data: Housing units without vehicles and housing units receiving SNAP.

Project file: Cuba_CCD_Food_Access_Cleaned.csv

[USDA – Food Access Research Atlas: Download the Data](https://www.ers.usda.gov/data-products/food-access-research-atlas/download-the-data)


* Graph 4 – Water Usage Comparison

Source: University of Colorado Denver, 2017 Research and Creative Activities Symposium, page 25.

Data: Water dependency comparison between conventional agriculture and aquaponics.

[University of Colorado Denver – 2017 Research and Creative Activities Symposium (PDF)](https://www.ucdenver.edu/docs/librariesprovider114/default-document-library/racas-2017-abstract.pdf)


* Graph 5 – Aquaponics Vegetable Production

Source: Goda et al. (2015), Table 2, page 531.

Data: Vegetable production rates for cucumber, tomato, and head lettuce in an aquaponics system.

[Goda et al. (2015) – Aquaponics Research Article (PDF)](https://www.trjfas.org/uploads/pdf_793.pdf)


* Graph 6 – Greenhouse Production Projections

Based on the production rates from Graph 5.

Greenhouse dimensions: 30 × 96 feet.

Estimated growing-space allocation: 64%.

Production values are calculated projections, not measured results.
